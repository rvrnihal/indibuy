# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all participants to adhere to this code of conduct in all interactions within the IndiBuy community, including but not limited to:

- Project repositories
- Issue trackers
- Discussion forums
- Chat channels
- Conferences and events
- Social media

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

### Be Respectful
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members

### Be Collaborative
- Work together to solve problems
- Share knowledge and help others learn
- Give credit to contributions
- Communicate clearly and directly
- Assume good intent

### Be Professional
- Keep discussions focused and on-topic
- Avoid unnecessary conflict
- Report issues through proper channels
- Respect confidentiality and privacy
- Maintain professionalism in all communications

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

### Harassment
- Harassment of any kind (sexual, racial, religious, etc.)
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Stalking or following
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Repeated unwanted contact

### Discrimination
- Discrimination based on age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political beliefs, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or any other protected characteristic

### Violence & Threats
- Violent threats or language
- Encouragement of violence
- Physical violence or threats
- Violent language directed at another person
- Imagery of violence

### Harmful Content
- Content that is illegal in the jurisdiction where posted
- Content promoting violence or self-harm
- Content promoting discrimination
- Content that violates privacy or intellectual property rights
- Spam or commercial solicitation

### Other Violations
- Deliberate misrepresentation
- Publishing private information without consent
- Abuse of power or privilege
- Retaliation for good-faith reporting

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the IndiBuy team:

- **Email**: conduct@indibuy.com
- **GitHub**: Private message to maintainers
- **Anonymous**: https://indibuy.com/report-conduct

When reporting, please include:
- Description of the incident
- People involved
- Context and any evidence
- Your contact information (optional)

All reports will be:
- Taken seriously
- Investigated promptly
- Handled with confidentiality
- Treated fairly and impartially

### Investigation & Response

Our team will:

1. **Acknowledge** receipt of the report within 24 hours
2. **Investigate** thoroughly and impartially
3. **Determine** appropriate response
4. **Notify** involved parties of decision
5. **Implement** corrective action
6. **Follow up** to ensure resolution

### Consequences

Violations may result in:
- **Warning** - First-time minor violations
- **Temporary Ban** - Repeated violations or serious incidents
- **Permanent Ban** - Severe or egregious violations
- **Legal Action** - If illegal behavior is involved

---

## Appeal Process

Individuals who have been sanctioned may appeal the decision:

1. Submit written appeal to conduct@indibuy.com
2. Explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered
3. Provide any additional context or evidence
4. Response will be provided within 7 days

Appeals will be reviewed by a neutral third party.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project repositories and code
- Issue trackers and pull requests
- Discussion forums and chat channels
- Official project events and conferences
- Social media when representing the project
- Any official communication channels

This Code of Conduct also applies outside these spaces when:
- Someone is representing the project
- Actions are related to the project
- The behavior affects the community's safety or well-being
